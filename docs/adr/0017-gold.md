# ADR 0017: Gold

Status: accepted (2026-09-28).

## Context

Many Turkish savers hold gram gold and coins (çeyrek, yarım, tam, ata).
gelirgider could only record it as the `PAXG-TRY` crypto pair, priced per
troy ounce, and gold could not be a benchmark ([ADR 0012](0012-benchmarks.md)).

## Decision

### Gram gold: market `GOLD`, symbol `GRAM`, in TRY

Gram gold is one instrument, `GOLD/GRAM`, named "Gram Altın", currency `TRY`.
The quantity is grams, so a buy of 12.5 g at 4,300 TRY is quantity 12.5,
price 4300.

`GOLD` is its own market, not a BIST instrument, so gold stays out of
everything keyed on market `BIST`: the BIST backfill, splits
([ADR 0008](0008-splits.md)) and dividends ([ADR 0009](0009-dividends.md)).
Search labels it like `CRYPTO` ([ADR 0007](0007-crypto.md)).

### Price: BtcTurk PAXG-TRY close / 31.1034768

PAXG is a token backed by one fine troy ounce of London gold (Paxos), and
BtcTurk trades it against TRY. One troy ounce is exactly 31.1034768 g (NIST
Handbook 44), so the gram price of day `d` is

```
gram_d = close(PAXGTRY, d) / 31.1034768
```

where `close` is the finished daily candle of the BtcTurk klines endpoint of
ADR 0007, stored under the candle's UTC date like every crypto price. The
division is `decimal`, never `float64` (PRINCIPLES #3). The `internal/btcturk`
fetcher is reused, a keyless source whose hosts publish no robots.txt rule
against it. The `PAXG-TRY` crypto instrument is unchanged and keeps its
per-ounce price.

A spike checked the proxy against the gram price implied by the BIST gold
certificates that track Borsa İstanbul's spot gold index. Over the 28 sampled
days since the certificates started trading (2024-11), the mean deviation was
+0.02% and 19 of 27 days were within 1%, the rest up to 3.2%, explained by
the UTC close being hours after the certificates stop trading, by the
certificates' yearly fee, and by a thin first month. Those certificates are
not a better source: they trade on BIST days only, mature and change code,
carry the fee in their price and start 28 months after PAXG, and Darphane's
`ALTIN.S1` carries a premium that reached 69% over PAXG. So the PAXG proxy
stands.

### Stored twice: `prices` and `benchmark_values`

Each day's gram price is written, from one computed value, both to `prices` for
`GOLD/GRAM` with source `btcturk_paxg`, so holdings, Özet and the portfolio
history value gram gold like any other instrument, and to `benchmark_values`
with code `GOLD`, so the benchmark reuses the XU100 and TÜFE code path. The
two series cannot differ.

### Scheduler source `gold`, every day

The `gold` source fetches the PAXG-TRY candles and writes both rows. It runs
every day, weekends included, like `crypto`, since PAXG trades every day.
Unlike `crypto`, `us` and `tefas` it does not skip when nobody holds the
instrument, because the benchmark needs the series either way. The startup and admin
backfill start at the `from` override, else the earliest transaction in any
market, and fetch nothing when there is neither; the start moves forward to
2022-06-30, BtcTurk's first PAXG-TRY candle, when it is earlier. While `gold`
is enabled, every transaction save in any market also fetches the series from
the transaction date, so a fresh database does not wait for a restart ([ADR 0019](0019-core-engine-and-auth.md)).

### GOLD, a fifth fixed benchmark

The benchmark selector has GOLD ("Altın (gram)" / "Gold (gram)") next to USD,
EUR, BIST 100 and TÜFE. It uses the ADR 0012 shadow portfolio unchanged: a
`TRY` level converted into the reporting currency like XU100, hidden for
ranges that start before the first stored gold value.

### Coins: `GOLD` instruments, manual prices only

Çeyrek, yarım, tam and ata altın are seeded as `GOLD` instruments in `TRY`,
quantity in pieces, with no fetched price. The user enters prices with the
existing manual prices. The price is not derived as gram price times fine gold
weight: a coin sells with a mint and jeweller premium that changes over time,
so a derived price would undervalue every coin.

## Recorded simplifications

- No fetched gold price before 2022-06-30; manual prices can fill those days.
- No bank buy/sell spread and no coin premium: gram gold is valued at the
  PAXG price.
- The UTC candle date of ADR 0007 applies: the close stored for day `d`
  happens at 03:00 Istanbul on `d + 1`.

## Not supported

Silver, platinum or other metals, gold priced in USD or per ounce as a `GOLD`
instrument, fetched coin prices or a coin price derived from gram gold, bank
gold accounts with the bank's spread, and gold broker statement import.
