# ADR 0017: Gold

Status: accepted (GLG-534, 2026-09-28); amended 2026-09-29 (GLG-541, the
spike confirms the BtcTurk PAXG-TRY source, see "Source")

## Context

Many Turkish savers hold gold: gram gold at a bank or a jeweller, and coins
(çeyrek, yarım, tam, ata). gelirgider cannot record it today except as the
`PAXG-TRY` crypto pair, which is priced per troy ounce, and gold is not a
benchmark. ADR 0012 left it out of Phase 13 ("Not in this epic: gold").

This ADR records the decisions of epic GLG-522 (Phase 18), taken with the
user on 2026-09-28.

## Decision

### Gram gold: market `GOLD`, symbol `GRAM`, in TRY

Gram gold is one instrument, `GOLD/GRAM`: market `GOLD`, symbol `GRAM`,
name "Gram Altın", currency `TRY`. The quantity is grams, so a buy of 12.5
g at 4,300 TRY is quantity 12.5, price 4300.

`GOLD` is a new market, not a BIST instrument. So gold stays out of
everything keyed on market `BIST`: the BIST backfill, splits (ADR 0008) and
dividends (ADR 0009). Instrument search labels the market like `CRYPTO`
(ADR 0007).

### Price: BtcTurk PAXG-TRY close ÷ 31.1034768

PAXG is a token backed by one troy ounce of London gold, and BtcTurk
trades it against TRY. The gram price of day `d` is

```
gram_d = close(PAXGTRY, d) / 31.1034768
```

where 31.1034768 is grams per troy ounce and `close` is the finished daily
candle of the BtcTurk klines endpoint of ADR 0007. The price is stored under
the candle's UTC date, like every crypto price (ADR 0007). The division is
done in `decimal`, never `float64` (PRINCIPLES #3).

This reuses the `internal/btcturk` fetcher, a keyless source already in
production. The `PAXG-TRY` crypto instrument is unchanged and keeps its
per-ounce price.

### Stored twice: `prices` and `benchmark_values`

Each day's gram price is written

- to `prices` for `GOLD/GRAM`, source `btcturk_paxg`, so holdings, Özet and
  the portfolio history value gram gold like any other instrument;
- to `benchmark_values` with code `GOLD`, so the benchmark reads the same
  table as XU100 and TÜFE and reuses their code path (ADR 0012, GLG-495).

Both writes happen in one sync, from one computed value, so the two series
cannot differ.

### Scheduler source `gold`, every day

A scheduler source `gold` fetches the PAXG-TRY candles and writes both
rows. It runs every day, weekends included, like `crypto` (ADR 0007), since
PAXG trades every day.

Unlike `crypto`, `us` and `tefas` it does not skip when nobody holds the
instrument: the benchmark needs the series whether or not anyone holds
gold. On startup and from the admin backfill it fills the history from
2022-06-30, and it is listed with the other sources in the admin sources
list.

> Amended 2026-10-02 (GLG-749): every transaction save, in any market, also
> fetches the series from the transaction date while `gold` is enabled
> (ADR 0019), so the GOLD benchmark no longer waits for a restart or an
> admin backfill on a fresh database.

### GOLD, a fifth benchmark

The benchmark selector gets a fifth option, GOLD ("Altın (gram)" / "Gold
(gram)"), next to USD, EUR, BIST 100 and TÜFE. It uses the ADR 0012 shadow
portfolio unchanged. One more row in its table:

| benchmark | currency | level          |
| --------- | -------- | -------------- |
| GOLD      | `TRY`    | the gram price |

It is converted into the reporting currency with the sweep's FX rates like
XU100. The ADR 0012 missing-data rule applies: a range that starts before
the first stored gold value hides the GOLD benchmark.

### Coins: `GOLD` instruments, manual prices only

Çeyrek, yarım, tam and ata altın are seeded as instruments in market
`GOLD`, currency `TRY`, quantity in pieces. They have no fetched price. The
user enters their prices with the existing manual prices.

Their price is not derived as gram price × fine gold weight. A coin sells
with a mint and jeweller premium over its gold content, so the derived
price would undervalue every coin, by an amount that changes over time.

### Source

Verified by the spike GLG-541 on 2026-09-29. The requests ran from a local
machine, since the cloud sandbox proxy refuses the BtcTurk hosts. The three
claims of the design pass hold, within the limits below, so the price
decision above stands.

```
GET https://graph-api.btcturk.com/v1/klines/history?symbol=PAXGTRY&resolution=1D&from=1483228800&to=1790672358
User-Agent: gelirgider/1.0 (+https://github.com/dogauzun/gelirgider)
```

- Response: 200, 134 KB of JSON in the ADR 0007 shape that
  `btcturk.Fetcher.FetchCandles` reads: `s` is `"ok"`, then six arrays of
  equal length, `t`, `h`, `o`, `l`, `c` and `v`. The fetcher uses `s`, `t`
  (00:00 UTC of the candle's day, in unix seconds) and `c` (the close, in
  TRY per PAXG).
- Pair: `PAXGTRY` has status `TRADING` in `exchangeinfo` (ADR 0007). Every
  close in the history is a whole number of TRY.
- History: one request returns all of it. The first candle is 2022-06-30
  (close 30550), the last 2026-09-29, which is the current, unfinished day.
  1553 rows, one per calendar day, with no gap, no duplicate and no empty or
  zero close. 444 rows are Saturdays or Sundays.

#### Factor

1 PAXG is one fine troy ounce of gold, and one troy ounce is exactly
31.1034768 g.

- Paxos, the issuer, at https://www.paxos.com/pax-gold (read 2026-09-29):
  "one token represents one fine troy ounce of a London Good Delivery gold
  bar".
- NIST Handbook 44, 2026 edition, Appendix C, table "Units of Mass", at
  https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.HB.44-2026 (read 2026-09-29): 1 troy ounce
  is 31.103 476 8 g, being 480 grains of exactly 64.798 91 mg.
- Borsa İstanbul's spot gold index below converts with the same factor,
  as 0.0321507465 ounces per gram.

So the gram price is 30550 / 31.1034768 = 982.21 TRY on 2022-06-30 and
202907 / 31.1034768 = 6523.61 TRY on 2026-09-28.

#### Against BIST gold certificates

Three brokers issue gold certificates that trade on BIST, in instrument
group `XCR` of the equity bulletin (ADR 0001): `AGXAA.C` (Ak Yatırım, first
in the bulletin on 2024-11-07), `IGXAA.C` (İş Yatırım, 2025-02-20) and
`1GXAA.C` (İnfo Yatırım, 2025-03-27, a digit one). All three track
`ALTSPT`, the BIST Spot Gold index, which Borsa İstanbul computes every 10
seconds from 10:00 to 18:05 as the XAU/USD spot mid times the USD/TRY mid
times 0.0321507465 ounces per gram (BIST's gold index rule set of December
2023,
https://borsaistanbul.com/files/bist-altina-dayali-endeksler-kural-seti-25122023.pdf).
So they check PAXG against the spot gram price; bank and jeweller prices
add their own spread. The issuers' documents, read 2026-09-29, give each
certificate a conversion ratio of 1/100, so 100 certificates are one gram,
and a 1% yearly management fee taken out of the price every week:

- Ak Yatırım: https://varant.akyatirim.com.tr/rehber/sertifika/sertifika-turleri/
- İş Yatırım: https://www.isyatirim.com.tr/tr-tr/analiz/arastirma-raporlari/ArastirmaRaporlari/6_20250221114653506_1.pdf
- İnfo Yatırım: https://infovarant.com/sertifikalar/sertifika-turleri

The certificate gram price is the bulletin close × 100. Each row takes the
certificate with the largest traded value that day and PAXG's close of the
same UTC date. Deviation is PAXG ÷ certificate - 1. `ALTIN.S1` is Darphane's
certificate, shown for its premium (below).

| Date       | PAXG TRY/g | Certificate | Certificate TRY/g | Deviation | `ALTIN.S1` × 100 |
| ---------- | ---------- | ----------- | ----------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| 2024-12-02 | 2943.43    | `AGXAA.C`   | 3010.00           | -2.21%    | 3245.00          |
| 2025-01-02 | 3012.49    | `AGXAA.C`   | 3000.00           | +0.42%    | 3079.00          |
| 2025-03-03 | 3392.16    | `IGXAA.C`   | 3373.00           | +0.57%    | 3575.00          |
| 2025-04-02 | 3913.61    | `IGXAA.C`   | 3791.00           | +3.23%    | 4292.00          |
| 2025-10-01 | 5163.12    | `1GXAA.C`   | 5160.00           | +0.06%    | 6540.00          |
| 2026-01-02 | 5996.11    | `AGXAA.C`   | 5997.00           | -0.01%    | 10123.00         |
| 2026-03-02 | 7556.68    | `1GXAA.C`   | 7578.00           | -0.28%    | 9386.00          |
| 2026-09-28 | 6523.61    | `1GXAA.C`   | 6473.00           | +0.78%    | 7139.00          |

The full sample is the first BIST day of each month from 2024-12 to
2026-09, 2026-09-28 and five days in the first four weeks of `AGXAA.C`. 27
of these 28 days have a certificate trade. The deviation runs from -2.21%
to +3.23%, the mean is +0.02%, the mean absolute deviation 0.83%, and 19 of
the 27 days are within 1%.

The larger gaps come from:

- Timing. The stored close is the 24:00 UTC price, nine hours after the
  certificates stop trading at 18:00 Istanbul. On 2025-04-02 gold rose in
  the last UTC hours: the hourly candles (`resolution=60`) put PAXG at
  3841.08 TRY/g at 15:00 UTC, 0.5% to 0.7% over the three certificates'
  end-of-day bid/ask mids (3815.50 to 3821.00), and the UTC day closed at
  3913.61. That day's certificate close is also thin: 8113 TRY traded.
- The certificate. PAXG was 0.13% to 2.21% below `AGXAA.C` on all six
  sampled days of the certificate's first four weeks, up to 2024-12-02.
  That day PAXG's own low and high were 2936.84 and 2955.88 TRY/g, so the
  gap is not timing. On its later high-volume days, 2025-01-02 and
  2026-01-02 in the table, it is in line.
- The fee. Certificate prices are net of the 1% yearly fee, roughly 1.3%
  of the price by 2026-09-28 for `1GXAA.C`. Against the index itself, the
  2026-09-28 row is about -0.5%, not +0.78%.

Before 2024-11-07 there is no gold certificate: none of the 12 bulletins
sampled from 2022-07-01 to 2024-11-06 has one. So the first 28 months of
the PAXG series have no Turkish spot quote to check against.

`ALTIN.S1` is 0.01 g of 995 gold per certificate, convertible to physical
gold (Borsa İstanbul,
https://www.borsaistanbul.com/en/participation-finance/turkish-state-mint-gold-certificate,
read 2026-09-29). It is absent from the 2022-11-01 bulletin and present on
2022-12-01. Its price is its own market: on the 39 sampled days from
2022-12-01 to 2026-09-28 it stood between 1.81% below (2024-06-03) and
68.83% above (2026-01-02) the PAXG gram price.

#### Claims

- PAXG-TRY ÷ 31.1034768 stays close to the TRY gram gold price quoted in
  Türkiye: **holds from 2024-11-08**, the first certificate trade. Within
  1% on most days, at most 3.23%, for the reasons above. Before that date
  there is nothing to check against.
- BIST gold certificates and `ALTIN.S1` would not give a better series:
  **holds**. They trade on BIST days only, with no weekend or holiday rows.
  The certificates start on 2024-11-07, 28 months after PAXG, they mature
  (`IGXAA.C` on 2027-02-26, `1GXAA.C` on 2027-03-26), so a long series
  would chain codes, their price carries the yearly fee, and a code often
  has no trade on a given day (`IGXAA.C` on every sampled day from
  2026-04-01). `ALTIN.S1` starts in November 2022 and carries a premium of
  its own.
- BtcTurk's first PAXG-TRY daily candle is 2022-06-30: **holds**.

robots.txt, checked 2026-09-29 with the project User-Agent. The BtcTurk
answers are the same as the 2026-09-23 record in ADR 0007:

- `graph-api.btcturk.com`, the host the fetcher calls: 404, no robots.txt.
- `www.btcturk.com`: `User-agent: *` / `Allow: /`. No group names an AI
  crawler.
- `api.btcturk.com` (`exchangeinfo`): 404. `www.borsaistanbul.com` (the
  bulletins): `User-agent: *` / `Allow: /`.

## Recorded simplifications

- No fetched gold price before 2022-06-30, the first PAXG-TRY candle. A buy
  before that shows its price as unknown. Manual prices can fill those days.
  The GOLD benchmark is hidden for ranges that start earlier (ADR 0012
  missing-data rule).
- No bank buy/sell spread and no coin premium: gram gold is valued at the
  PAXG mid price, and coins at whatever the user enters.
- The same series is stored twice, in `prices` and in `benchmark_values`,
  so the benchmark needs no new code path.
- The UTC candle date of ADR 0007 applies: the close stored for day `d`
  happens at 03:00 Istanbul on `d + 1`.

### Not in this epic

- Silver, platinum or other metals.
- Gold priced in USD or per ounce as a `GOLD` instrument.
- Fetched coin prices or a coin price derived from gram gold.
- Bank gold accounts as their own instrument, with the bank's spread.
- Gold broker statement import.

## Consequences

- GLG-534 writes this ADR and the Phase 18 roadmap section.
- GLG-541 confirms PAXG-TRY against BIST gold certificates and `ALTIN.S1`
  and fills "Source" above.
- GLG-544 adds the `GOLD` market with the Gram Altın instrument and labels

- GLG-548 adds `?benchmark=GOLD` to the history API and the GOLD option to
  the chart header.
- GLG-550 adds `btcturk.SyncGold`, which stores the PAXG-TRY gram price in
  `prices` and `benchmark_values`.
- GLG-555 adds the `gold` scheduler source, its startup backfill and its
  entry in the admin sources list.
- GLG-556 seeds the gold coins as manually priced `GOLD` instruments.
