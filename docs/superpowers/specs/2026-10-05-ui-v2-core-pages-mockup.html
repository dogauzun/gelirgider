Rules behind "Dikkat" (defaults; say if you want other numbers)
  • Veri: a position without a price; a currency without an FX rate; a manual price older than 7 days; a benchmark value that is an estimate.
  • Temettü onayı: every expected dividend not yet recorded or dismissed, with Ekle / Yoksay.
  • Bugün: the two positions that moved the portfolio most today, up or down, by amount.
  • Yoğunlaşma: one position above 25% of the total; cash above 40%; assets in one foreign currency above 50%. Shown as facts, no advice.
  • A group with nothing to show is hidden. With nothing at all, the card says "Her şey yolunda" and stays small.
